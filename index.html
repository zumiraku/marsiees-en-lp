Why Japan is different

Great products still stall in Japan. Usually for the same four reasons.

Japan is one of the largest B2B markets in the world, but the way B2B deals get done here catches many overseas teams off guard.

  • 01

    Business customs you can't learn from a guidebook

    Consensus-based decisions, long approval chains, introductions over cold pitches, formal keigo and business card etiquette. Small missteps quietly end conversations.

  • 02

    Nobody on your team can make the calls

    Most Japanese decision makers expect to be approached in Japanese, by phone, by someone who sounds like they belong in the room.

  • 03

    Trade show leads go cold after you fly home

    You collect a stack of business cards in Tokyo, then follow-up emails from overseas get ignored. And the window when interest is warm closes fast.

  • 04

    Hiring a local sales team is slow and risky

    Recruiting experienced B2B sales staff in Japan can take months, and you still don't know if the market will respond.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to speak Japanese to work with you?

No. Our team handles all Japanese-language calls and follow-up with your prospects. You can contact us in English through the form below.

What is the minimum contract period?

You can start with a 1-month trial. Because scripts, lists and results improve with every cycle, we recommend 3 months or longer.

Do we need to provide a call list?

If you have one, we can use it. If not, we will ask about your target criteria and build the list for you.

Who will actually handle our campaign?

Experienced female sales professionals. We never assign inexperienced staff to client projects.

What is the difference between telemarketing and sales outsourcing?

Telemarketing focuses on booking appointments by phone. Sales outsourcing covers everything from appointments to sales meetings and closing. We offer both, and they can be combined.

Does cold calling still work in Japan?

Yes. In Japanese B2B sales, the phone is still one of the most effective ways to reach decision makers directly, hear their challenges and turn that into a meeting.

If we outsource, will the know-how stay with us?

Yes. Daily logs, weekly reports and regular reviews share what worked in the scripts and calls, so the know-how builds up inside your company.
