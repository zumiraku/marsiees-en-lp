Why Japan is different

Great products still stall in Japan. Usually for the same four reasons.

Japan is one of the largest B2B markets in the world, but the way B2B deals get done here catches many overseas teams off guard.

  • 01

    Business customs you can't learn from a guidebook

    Consensus-based decisions, long approval chains, introductions over cold pitches, formal keigo and business card etiquette. Small missteps quietly end conversations.

  • 02

    Nobody on your team can make the calls

    Most Japanese decision makers expect to be approached in Japanese, by phone, by someone who sounds like they belong in the room.

  • 03

    Trade show leads go cold after you fly home

    You collect a stack of business cards in Tokyo, then follow-up emails from overseas get ignored. And the window when interest is warm closes fast.

  • 04

    Hiring a local sales team is slow and risky

    Recruiting experienced B2B sales staff in Japan can take months, and you still don't know if the market will respond.
